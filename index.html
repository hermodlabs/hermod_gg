01

Start with the composition

What does Varus bring to this team?

Aatrox, Master Yi, Orianna, and Shen are already selected. Across the map are Renekton, Qiyana, Syndra, Xayah, and Soraka. The useful question is what Varus contributes alongside these allies, against these opponents.

The contribution

Support the team’s damage window.

In this report, Varus’s Living Vengeance is linked to offensive-stat enhancement and the job of maintaining damage uptime within Protect and Convert. The model traces a champion mechanic to a team-level purpose.

The counterplay

Soraka can interfere with that same job.

The report flags conditional pressure when the damage source can be disrupted during the window the team needs. That gives the team something specific to check: can it keep the damage contribution available long enough to convert the play?

02

Change one choice

“What if we picked Ashe instead?”

Keep the same four allies and five enemies. Change the bottom-lane pick. Hermod re-examines how that choice contributes to the composition and where the opponent can interfere.

A different contribution

Information becomes part of the plan.

The Ashe report highlights Hawkshot’s scouting and connects it to converting a removal in Breach and Remove. The question becomes whether that information is relevant to the route or area where the team wants to secure value.

Recheck the resistance

The opponent stays. The relationship changes.

Soraka is still flagged, now under a condition concerning enemy control of the objective entrance or contest position. Replacing a champion means reassessing the jobs and exposures that matter to the new option.

This is counterfactual reasoning.

Hold the surrounding composition fixed. Change a candidate. Compare what the team gains, what must hold, and what can interfere. Both screenshots show conditional support and an additional provider; the decision comes from understanding which job each pick supports.

Repeat the comparison with available, role-valid champions in the app. Choose based on the strategic contribution your team wants to preserve or develop.
