What else could have worked? We have one concrete way to connect the team. Now we can keep the pathway and ask what another champion would change. For this exercise, we will explore replacing Graves with Ivern while preserving the goal of protected attack time and a supported Dragon attempt.

A pathway is abstract: it describes required jobs and the relationships between them. The champions and abilities are concrete ways to supply those jobs under particular conditions. Several champions can contribute to the same requirement through different methods. A replacement can therefore preserve recorded coverage while changing how the team must perform the work.

Keep the pathway requirementThe original example's assignment
Hold a usable formationGnar threatens the front; Viktor helps control the entrance.
Preserve attack timeLulu protects Jinx from a position where she can help.
Apply sustained pressureJinx and Graves use the reachable attack window.
Convert into DragonKeep damage on the objective and cover the remaining threats.

This is counterfactual reasoning: hold the intended result and selected conditions fixed, change one pick, and inspect the consequences. Composition Decision Lab compares recorded support, methods, and conditions. The maps illustrate the scenario; their positions and assumed fight results are not inputs to the assessment. The exercise leaves room for several useful alternatives, with no requirement to declare a winner.

Work through five steps: keep the situation fixed, state the plans, inspect who supplies the jobs, compare one replacement, and audit what the difference means. The existing screenshots show this exact lab setup. The link at the end opens it for your own exploration.

3. Inspect Who Supplies the Jobs

Read Where does this team have only one recorded provider? In the current model, the job Preserve priority asset, which requires Ally Protection, has one qualifying champion provider in this Blue lineup: Lulu.

Her two recorded methods are Help, Pix! and Wild Growth. That is two methods on one champion. In the connected scenario, Lulu has a protection assignment near Jinx. The report identifies the recorded methods supporting that assignment; it leaves their availability, delivery, and usefulness to be checked.

This gives us a specific dimension to explore. Another pick might add a provider for this exact job even when the original arrangement is acceptable. Concentration on Lulu identifies a dependency; it does not establish that the plan is broken or that every lineup needs a second protection provider.

Also read Where does this contribution matter? and What does the rest of this composition cover and leave open? Those panels show Graves's existing responsibilities and the rest of the route. Adding support to one requirement may change the methods available for another. Checkpoint: “We are exploring another way to supply Ally Protection within the same pathway. We will keep Graves's other contributions in view as we compare.”

4. Compare One Replacement

Under Compare the selected champion, leave Improvement to investigate on Diagnose automatically. With this setup and the current model, the diagnosis chooses Reduce provider concentration and displays Ivern, Nidalee, and Kindred. Ivern is selected automatically. The shortlist offers starting points for inspection; its structural ranking leaves matchup suitability and execution to be assessed.

The useful observation is specific: the protection job has another recorded champion provider while all six modeled jobs retain some conditional support. That demonstrates how a pathway can admit different contributors. It does not establish equal damage, equal objective speed, or interchangeable methods. Two protection providers may also depend on the same timing or access.

Open What does this comparison establish? and inspect the other changes. Graves's methods leave this version; Ivern brings his own. Ask how the remaining damage, entrance cover, and protection assignments would connect to Dragon. Jungle matchup and player familiarity also remain relevant. The comparison can reveal a tradeoff worth exploring without resolving which lineup to choose.

5. Audit the Evidence and Keep Exploring

Use Inspect evidence, Conditions apply, or What was checked beside the relevant finding. Follow Ivern's Triggerseed contribution through its mechanical tool and effects to the Ally Protection job. Do the same for Lulu's methods. Check the required recipient and conditions instead of stopping at the method count.
